Where we have been

We were here before the world arrived.

Mwanga entered the BPO market in Lagos before it was fashionable, before global capital arrived, before Nigeria cracked the world's top ten outsourcing destinations. We did not follow the trend. We were the trend.

✦
Day One
One founder. Female. Solo. Starts Mwanga in Lagos.
No co-founder. No institutional capital. No safety net. A female founder builds an ethical debt collection company in a category dominated by aggressive, male-led operations — and leads it with a standard of professionalism and governance that global companies are only now starting to demand. Every decision from day one has been hers alone. Every risk carried by one person. That is the foundation everything else is built on.
2
Growth Phase
Client base expands — fintechs, banks, commercial companies
Mwanga became the trusted partner for Nigeria's most regulated industries. Debt collection, customer support, inbound and outbound operations. Real clients. Real revenue. Real operational infrastructure built from the ground up.
3
2021
Yapha Academy launches — training African talent for the digital economy
Mwanga started training young Africans — in telecollections, customer support, telesales, negotiation, and digital skills. Eight cohorts. Over 1,000 learners. Thirteen countries. This was not a side project. It was a signal of what Mwanga understood before others did: the talent is here. It just needs the right infrastructure.

Today
Eight years in — and the world has finally caught up
Nigeria just entered the global top 10 outsourcing destinations for the first time. The global BPO market is heading towards $500 billion by 2030. The companies that came after us are scaling fast. And Mwanga — the pioneer — is at an inflection point. This document is about what we do next.
02
The honest reckoning

We have to say what is true.

We will not ask anyone to build with us on a foundation of optimism alone. Before we talk about the future, we name the present clearly.

What is true
We built something that survived
Eight years. Real operations. Real clients in regulated industries. A training programme that has touched over a thousand lives across thirteen African countries. Not many BPOs — in Nigeria or anywhere — can say that. Mwanga is still standing because it was built right.
What is also true
We hit a plateau
The same scrappiness and founder-led intensity that built Mwanga has also become the ceiling. The structure that worked in year two does not automatically scale to year ten. We have been running the business without fully building toward the next version of it. That changes now.
The founding truth
Bootstrapped. From day one.
Every client won on merit. Every salary paid from revenue earned. Every decision made without a funding round to fall back on. While others scaled on investor capital, Mwanga built on discipline. That is not a gap in our story. That is proof that the model works — and that what comes next is built on a foundation nobody can take away.
What the market did
Later entrants scaled faster
Companies that entered after us — with capital, with global positioning, with technology infrastructure — have grown quickly in a market we helped build. That is not a reason for bitterness. It is a reason for clarity about what we need to do differently in the next phase.
Why this moment is different
The window is open right now
Nigeria entering the global top 10 is not background noise. It is the moment Mwanga has been positioned for since day one. We have eight years of credibility that no new entrant can buy. The question is whether we use this window — or watch it close.

The technology layer

A BPO without walls needs intelligent infrastructure.

The vision of a borderless, remote-first operations company is only possible with the right technology underneath it. This is what we are building toward.

Phase 1 — Now
Integrated tools stack
Configure best-in-class existing tools — remote monitoring, client dashboards, LMS for Yapha, CRM for operations — into a coherent Mwanga stack. Operational in 90 days. No custom build required.
Phase 2 — Months 4–9
First proprietary layer
Build the unified client dashboard and the Yapha-to-live-project connector. Agents log in, clients see real-time performance. Yapha interns work on live Mwanga client projects from within the platform.
Phase 3 — Month 12+
The Mwanga OS
A purpose-built smart operations platform — monitoring, reporting, talent matching, client visibility, and community intelligence data capture — in one proprietary system. This is the technology moat.
10x
revenue potential when the OS becomes a licensable product
AI Integration
Human + AI, not human vs AI
AI handles first-contact interactions, compliance checking, sentiment analysis, and escalation routing. Human agents handle complexity, empathy, and relationship. Mwanga agents become the most effective financial operations specialists in the African BPO market.
